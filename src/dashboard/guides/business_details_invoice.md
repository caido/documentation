# Updating Billing and Invoice Information

After subscribing to any plan, you can manage your billing information and invoices through the Paddle customer portal.

Visit [https://dashboard.caido.io](https://dashboard.caido.io), authenticate with your account, and visit the [**Billing**](https://dashboard.caido.io/billing) page.

## Updating Billing Information

Click the `Update billing information` button to open the Paddle customer portal. Update your billing details in the panel that appears and save your changes.

<img alt="Update billing information" src="/_images/subscription_update_billing.png" center />

## Viewing and Updating Invoices

In the Paddle customer portal, close the billing information panel to access the portal overview. Navigate to the **Payments** section to view your payment history, then select the invoice you want to view or update.

<img alt="Payment history" src="/_images/subscription_payment_page.png" center />

On the selected invoice, click `Update Invoice` to open a panel where you can update your invoice information.

<img alt="Individual payment details" src="/_images/subscription_payment_details.png" center />

Update the relevant details and save your changes.

<img alt="Update invoice information" src="/_images/subscription_update_invoice.png" center />
